# Evidence and interpretation ledger

The earlier post hoc claim that 23 profile-by-drug cells were confirmed by the literature has been
retired. The cited omnibus sources do not directly validate 23 distinct coefficients.

## Current evidence rule

The 5 by 26 map is interpreted from three model-based quantities.

- Marginal soft support determines whether a cell is displayed. Cells below the prespecified
  support threshold are suppressed.
- A full-pipeline patient bootstrap provides an interval for each supported coefficient.
- Sign agreement across full-pipeline resamples describes directional stability.

Of 130 cells, 104 meet the marginal support gate and 26 are suppressed. Among supported cells, 37
bootstrap intervals exclude zero and 69 retain the same sign in at least 80 percent of resamples.
No regimen component passes the separate conditional-support gate after recorded context and
co-prescription are considered.

These quantities support hypothesis generation only. They do not establish causal efficacy,
comparative effectiveness, safety, or a treatment recommendation.

## Clinical literature

Literature is used only to provide background for resistance, regimen composition, HIV,
comorbidity, nutrition, bacillary burden, and regional care patterns. It is not used as a
retrospective confirmation label for learned cells.

## Terminology

Use `context-conditioned coefficient`, `regimen-outcome association`, or `response profile`.
Do not use `individual treatment effect`, `helps`, `hurts`, or an equivalent causal phrase for
the observational coefficient map.

Cycloserine and terizidone remain separate recorded variables. A source discussing one is not
automatically evidence for the other.

## Reproduction

The source values and uncertainty flags are aggregate outputs of
`scripts/run_shared_profile_full_pipeline_stability.py`. The public heatmap is
rendered by `figures/release/render_aggregate_figures.py`.

The retired literature-triage table is not a submission artifact and must not be regenerated by a
legacy entry point.
